How This Works

This ledger tracks conduct during Violeta Rosa sessions. Strikes escalate through four tiers, shown above, from a verbal warning up to a HICOM referral.

Roles

HICOM

Logs new infractions, and can edit or delete any entry — all from one access code-protected page.

Players

Can check their own record — strikes on file and current standing — using their name and the Record ID sent to them by a leader.

What Happens When You're Cited

  1. A HICOM member logs the infraction, noting what happened and when.
  2. The ledger calculates your current strike count and standing automatically.
  3. You're sent a short message with the infraction, the consequence, and your Record ID.
  4. You can check your full record anytime using that ID on the "Check My Record" page.
Record IDs are private — don't share yours with anyone outside the house.
